# HiramShot design system

Applied skills: UI/UX Pro Max and frontend-design. The final direction follows the user-provided Gemini specification and subsequent fixed-rate confirmation.

Audience: travelers and creators. Main task: choose real rental gear, see exact rates, and complete the five-step rental process.
Palette: matte charcoal #1E1E1E, crisp white #FFFFFF, sunset orange #FF5A1F, neutral surface #F4F4F2, secondary text #5C5C5C. Orange buttons use dark text for contrast. Flat geometry; no heavy gradients or drop shadows.
Type: Outfit for display and headings, Work Sans for body and utility labels.
Layout: spacious two-column hero, date controls, dynamic equipment grid, actual kit footage, five-step guide, FAQs, and policies. Mobile uses a single column, collapsible navigation, and stacked booking panels. The calculator stays visible above the mobile selection form and beside the desktop form.
Signature: the original HiramShot collection framed as a camera viewfinder, paired with the English brand line: Borrow the gear. Own the moment.
Brand: original HS artwork, orange monogram accent, wordmark, and proportions are preserved. Original untouched source is public/assets/official-logo.png; the existing workspace logo export is used in the interface and agreement PDF.
Media: actual kit photographs and silent product clips from the supplied folder. The collection visual reuses prior campaign artwork; its Filipino text is cropped from the interface. No invented reviews or customer counts.
Interaction: equipment/category filters, compatible package and add-on choices, duration pricing, combo discount, privacy consent, staged identity verification, typed or drawn signature, owner MFA and state-based actions. Dates use Manila calendar boundaries. Reduced motion is respected and interactive elements have focus indicators.
Verified browser widths: 375/390px phone, 768px tablet, 1440px desktop. No horizontal document overflow in tested phone/tablet views. Screenshots are in the ignored outputs folder.
