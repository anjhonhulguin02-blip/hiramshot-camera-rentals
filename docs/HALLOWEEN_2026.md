# Halloween campaign retired

The owner withdrew the Halloween campaign on November 2, 2026 (Philippine time), superseding the earlier November 3 end date. The original cinematic storefront, price dialogs and regular rate schedule are restored. New booking requests use the regular server-side calculator regardless of pickup date, client-supplied promotion fields or a previously open promo page. A stale client total is rejected with a refresh/review message.

The seasonal components, advertising, clock and promotional price calculation have been removed. Base rates, add-ons, deposits and existing camera + drone combo rules are unchanged. Existing booking/contract snapshots are preserved; their saved promotion metadata remains readable without enabling new discounts. Owner Gmail notifications and their database migration remain active independently.
